# 02 Employee Data — Learning Path

The heart of Employee Central. If you know this folder cold, you can hold a conversation with any HR stakeholder and any interviewer.

| # | File | What you get out of it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Person, User and Employment](01_Person_User_Employment_Model.md) | The three-layer model and why it exists — the #1 EC concept |
| 2 | [Effective dating and history](02_Effective_Dating_and_History.md) | Insert vs Correct, time slices, future dating, "as of" reads |
| 3 | [Biographical and Personal Information](03_Biographical_and_Personal_Information.md) | `personInfo` vs `personalInfo`, name formats, global info |
| 4 | [Addresses, contact and national ID](04_Addresses_Contact_and_National_ID.md) | Country-specific address formats, email/phone, ID card types |
| 5 | [Employment Information](05_Employment_Information.md) | Hire date, seniority, termination, the employment record itself |
| 6 | [Job Information](06_Job_Information.md) | The most important element in EC — every field explained |
| 7 | [Compensation Information](07_Compensation_Information.md) | Pay components, recurring vs non-recurring, FTE and annualisation |
| 8 | [Job relationships and dependants](08_Job_Relationships_and_Dependants.md) | Matrix managers, HR business partner, family members |
| 9 | [Employee data imports](09_Employee_Data_Imports.md) | The import templates, sequence, and how loads actually fail |
| — | [Interview Q&A](Interview_Questions_and_Answers.md) | Questions covering this whole folder |

**Milestone:** on a whiteboard, draw the Person → Employment → Job Information structure for someone who was hired in 2019, promoted in 2022, terminated in 2023 and rehired in 2025 — and say which IDs stay the same and which change.
