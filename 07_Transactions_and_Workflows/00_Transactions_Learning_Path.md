# 07 Transactions and Workflows — Learning Path

This folder is the **employee lifecycle**: what happens when someone joins, moves, is promoted, goes on assignment, leaves, and comes back — and how those changes get approved.

Everything you learned in folders 02–06 comes together here. A transaction is a data model, foundation objects, business rules, effective dating and permissions, all firing at once.

| # | File | What you get out of it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Events and Event Reasons](01_Events_and_Event_Reasons.md) | The objects that record *what happened*, and the employee status they drive |
| 2 | [Hire and rehire](02_Hire_and_Rehire.md) | The hire process end to end, pending hires, and what rehire really creates |
| 3 | [Job change, transfer, promotion](03_Job_Change_Transfer_Promotion.md) | The everyday transactions and their variants |
| 4 | [Termination](04_Termination.md) | Termination, its many dates, downstream effects and data retention |
| 5 | [Global assignment and concurrent employment](05_Global_Assignment_and_Concurrent_Employment.md) | The two multi-employment scenarios, in detail |
| 6 | [Workflow fundamentals](06_Workflow_Fundamentals.md) | Pending data, approver types, CC roles, delegation, escalation |
| 7 | [Workflow configuration step by step](07_Workflow_Configuration_Step_by_Step.md) | Building a real two-step workflow with derivation |
| 8 | [Workflow administration and troubleshooting](08_Workflow_Admin_and_Troubleshooting.md) | Stuck workflows, reassignment, mass actions, the leaver problem |
| — | [Interview Q&A](Interview_Questions_and_Answers.md) | Questions covering this whole folder |

**Milestone:** in a practice system, hire someone, promote them through a two-step workflow with a derived event reason, terminate them, and rehire them — then explain every record created, every rule that fired, and every approval that routed, without looking at notes.
